# Communication Guidelines

**Status:** Recommendation (draft for members). Channel names `TBD`.

## Purpose

Make it safe to be a beginner, and make it easy to get a useful answer. Most community failure is a communication failure, not a content failure.

## Principles

1. **Ask the real question.** Include what you tried, what you expected, what happened.
2. **Answer the question asked.** Do not redirect to a tutorial unless the person asked for one.
3. **No "just Google it".** If you know the answer, write two sentences.
4. **Credit what you learned.** Link the source that helped.
5. **Disagree with the idea.** Never with the person.
6. **Say when you don't know.** "I'm not sure, but here's how I'd find out" is useful.

## Asking a good question

```text
What I'm trying to do:
What I tried:
What I expected:
What actually happened (error text or screenshot):
```

## Giving feedback

Use the three-part structure: one thing that works, one thing unclear, one thing to try next. Ask before giving unsolicited critique on someone's personal project.

## Language

English is the working language. Non-native English is normal and never corrected unless asked. Members may write in their own language when helping someone who shares it.

## Boundaries

- No harassment, discrimination, or sexual content.
- No spam, recruitment, course selling, or self-promotion without permission.
- No sharing another member's private information or work without consent.
- No political or religious campaigning.
- No medical, legal or financial advice presented as professional advice.
- Minors: no private one-to-one contact between adults and minors; use public channels.

## Moderation (proposed)

1. Private, specific message to the member.
2. Public reminder of the guideline if behaviour continues.
3. Temporary removal from the channel.
4. Removal from the community.

Severe cases (harassment, safety risk) skip to removal and are logged. Who moderates, and how reports are received, is `TBD`.

## Response expectations

The team does not promise a response time. Members are asked to be patient; the team commits to reading everything. Once a real capacity is known, publish an honest expected response window.

## Announcements

- Short, dated, UTC.
- One message per topic.
- State what changed and whether action is needed.
- No engagement bait, no "drop a 🔥 if…".
