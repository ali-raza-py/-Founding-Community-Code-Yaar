# Member Onboarding

**Status:** Recommendation. Channels, tools and dates are `TBD`.

## Goals

By the end of onboarding a new member should know:

1. What this community is and is not.
2. Where the conversation happens.
3. What the first thing they can do is.
4. Where to ask a question without embarrassment.
5. How to give feedback and how to leave.

## Sequence

| # | Step | Owner | Timing | Content |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Selection notification | Review team | `TBD` | Accepted / waitlisted / declined; next steps; no dates presented as guaranteed |
| 2 | Welcome message | CGO (Shabbar) | Within 3 days of acceptance (`TBD`) | Purpose, expectations, what is optional |
| 3 | Community guidelines | CGO | With welcome | Respect, no spam, no harassment, privacy, beginner-friendly language |
| 4 | Introduction | Member | First week | Name, what you're learning, one thing you want to build |
| 5 | Channel access | CGO | With welcome | Primary channel only; second channels added later if justified |
| 6 | First activity | Team | Within two weeks (`TBD`) | Small, time-boxed, async, no prerequisite |
| 7 | Feedback prompt | Team | Two weeks after joining | 4 questions: what worked, what didn't, what you need, what you'd change |

## Guardian consent for minors

If the member is under 18, onboarding **must not begin** until the guardian-consent process is complete. Process `TBD`; see [`../06-privacy-and-safety/APPLICANT-SAFETY.md`](../06-privacy-and-safety/APPLICANT-SAFETY.md).

## Communication defaults

- One primary channel. Do not split attention across three platforms at launch.
- Announcements are short and dated (UTC).
- Questions are answered in public where possible so others benefit.
- Private messages are for personal or safety matters only.

## First 30 days

| Week | Expectation |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Introduction posted; guidelines read |
| 2 | Attempts the first activity (or explicitly defers) |
| 3 | Responds to one other member's work |
| 4 | Sends the feedback prompt response |

"Attempts/responds" are invitations, not obligations. Members who are silent for 30 days receive a friendly check-in, not a warning.

## Recognition

Founding-member recognition is tied to participation, is non-competitive, and has no agreed format yet (badge, site listing, nothing). Do not promise a specific artefact.

## Offboarding

A member can leave at any time and ask for deletion of their application data. Deletion procedure depends on the chosen provider; tracked as `TBD` in [`../06-privacy-and-safety/DATA-RETENTION.md`](../06-privacy-and-safety/DATA-RETENTION.md).
