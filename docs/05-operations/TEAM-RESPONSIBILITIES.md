## Decision rights

- **Public promises** (benefits, certificate policy, fees, dates): founder approval required.
- **Technical architecture within the frontend-only constraint**: CTO.

## Capacity reality

This is a small team with a platform under development. The community site is intentionally static and low-maintenance. Any operational commitment that requires daily attention should be challenged before it is announced.

## Escalation

Safety or privacy incidents: notify Ali Raza immediately and stop processing the affected data. Do not handle a safeguarding report through a public channel.
