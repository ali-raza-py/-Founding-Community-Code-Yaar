# Founding Cohort Strategy

## Program name
The First 100 · Global Founding Cohort 2026–27

## What it is

The initial global founding community around Code Yaar — a small group of learners and aspiring developers who join before the platform is finished.

## Member journey

Apply → Review → Welcome → Connect → Learn → Build → Contribute → Showcase

## Benefits (honest, no overpromise)

- Early access to selected community experiences
- A say in early product decisions
- Founding community discussions
- Challenges and collaborative activities (opt-in, as available)
- Project and progress showcases
- Founding recognition, subject to participation (format not yet confirmed)

## Explicitly not promised

- Jobs, internships, scholarships, professional certifications
- Membership as a credential
- Guaranteed acceptance into any future programme

## Status

Early stage. Community being assembled now; activities will grow with the people in it.
