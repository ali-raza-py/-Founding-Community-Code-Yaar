# FAQ and Messaging

**Status:** Implemented on `/faq`. Answers reflect current knowledge and must be updated when founders decide open questions.

## Canvas

The FAQ is the trust page. A sceptical reader arrives with three worries: *is this real, is it free, will it help me get a job*. Each answer must resolve the worry without inflating the offer.

## Questions and approved answers

### 1. What is The First 100?

The first 100 members of the Code Yaar community: a founding cohort of learners and aspiring developers who participate in practical activities, give feedback, and help set the tone for everyone who joins later. It is early — the first group is being assembled now.

### 2. Is the cohort free?

No fee is being charged for the founding cohort. (Founder confirmation of the final policy is still recorded as pending in the decision log; the public answer states the current intention.) Members are not paid either.

### 3. Can applicants from any country apply?

Yes. Applications are open worldwide, and activities are designed to work across time zones, mostly asynchronously. The community is new, so the founding cohort is the first international group rather than an existing global network.

### 4. Do I need programming experience?

No. Beginners are the core audience. Interest and consistency matter more than experience. Being able to say what you want to learn is more useful than listing technologies.

### 5. Is this only for university students?

No. University students, school students, bootcamp learners, self-taught developers and people outside formal education are all welcome. The form includes "Self-taught" and "Not currently studying".

### 6. How are members selected?

Applications are read by the team against motivation, willingness to learn, interest in practical work, collaboration, and realistic availability. Existing skill level, follower counts, institution names and country are not selection criteria. Exact review turnaround is not yet published.

### 7. How much time should I contribute?

There is no quota and no daily requirement. Most members should expect a few hours a week at most, and the form asks for honest availability. A member with two realistic hours is more useful than one who plans twenty and disappears.

### 8. Will activities be available across time zones?

Activities are designed to be asynchronous-first, with optional live sessions in rotating time windows where they happen. No activity will require attendance at a fixed local hour. A published calendar does not exist yet.

### 9. Will I receive a certificate?

A certificate is not currently part of the founding cohort. If that changes it will be stated on this site and in the cohort details before anyone is asked to rely on it. Founding membership itself is not a credential or certification.

### 10. Does membership guarantee an internship or job?

No. Code Yaar makes no employment, internship, scholarship or career-outcome promise. The value offered is practice, peers, feedback and early involvement — not placement.

### 11. How will selected applicants be contacted?

By email, using the address in the application. Sender identity, timeline, and the notification template are `TBD` and will be confirmed before applications close. No decisions are communicated through social media.

### 12. Can I apply without a GitHub account?

Yes. GitHub, LinkedIn and a portfolio are optional and are never used to filter applicants. Many founding members will be at the stage where those profiles do not exist yet.

## Messaging do / don't

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| "Applications are open worldwide." | "Join developers from 50+ countries." |
| "We plan to run practical activities." | "Weekly challenges every Monday." |
| "Founding members help shape the community." | "Shape the future of education." |
| "No fee is being charged." | "100% free forever." |
| "Advanced experience is not required." | "Anyone can become a senior developer." |
