# Community Strategy

## Cohort size

One hundred founding members, filled through open applications rather than invitations.

## Selection criteria

Motivation, willingness to learn, interest in practical work, collaboration, and realistic availability. Existing skill level, follower counts, institution names, and country are not selection criteria.

## Global participation

Applications welcome from anywhere. Activities designed to be asynchronous-first; optional live sessions paired with an asynchronous path. English is the working language; preferred communication language is collected to plan support, not to filter applicants.

## Member expectations

- Participate constructively and respect other members
- Share learning experiences, including parts that did not work
- Give useful feedback — specific, not just encouraging
- Attempt practical activities at your own pace
- Communicate honestly when you cannot take part
- Contribute according to realistic availability

## What we do not require

- No mandatory daily hours or live-session attendance
- No prior programming experience
- No GitHub, LinkedIn, or portfolio (all optional)

## Communication

Selected applicants contacted by email. No decisions communicated through social media.
