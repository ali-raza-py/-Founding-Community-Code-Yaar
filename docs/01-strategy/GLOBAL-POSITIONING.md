# Global Positioning

**Status:** Confirmed ambition; all specifics below are honest-stage constraints.

## The position

The First 100 is open to applicants **anywhere in the world**. It is not a Pakistan-only initiative and must never read like one.

At the same time, the community is **new**. It has no established international membership, no partner organizations, and no events. The site must hold both truths at once: *global by design, honest about being early.*

## The honest-stage rule

| Never say | Say instead |
| --- | --- |
| "Our members come from 40+ countries." | "The founding cohort is being built from scratch, starting with this first group." |
| "Join our global network of thousands." | "Applications are open worldwide." |
| "We partner with universities across Asia and Africa." | (omit — no partnerships exist) |
| "Attend our international summits." | "Activities are planned to work across time zones, mostly asynchronously." |
| "Trusted by developers in 120 countries." | (omit — no trust claims without evidence) |

## Regional welcome

The site should make a visitor in Lagos, Lahore, Jakarta, Cairo, São Paulo, Berlin or Toronto feel addressed, without pretending each region already has a chapter.

Recommended techniques (implemented):

- Neutral, non-Western example framing in copy (no "Silicon Valley" defaults).
- Country field with a complete, searchable list rather than a short curated one.
- Time zone field capturing `UTC±` offsets rather than assuming a single working day.
- Explicit statements that live sessions are optional and recorded/asynchronous alternatives are preferred.
- No flag imagery, no country counter, no "regions we serve" grid that implies chapters.

## Time zones

Design commitments:

1. Core activities are **asynchronous-first**: written prompts, threads, asynchronous review.
2. Any live session is **optional**, scheduled in rotating windows, and paired with an async path.
3. Deadlines are stated with an explicit UTC time.
4. No activity requires attendance at a fixed local hour.

This is a design principle for operations; it is not a claim that the schedule exists yet.

## Language

English is the working language of the site and cohort. Members may use other languages in conversation; the form asks for a preferred communication language so the team can avoid excluding people. Do not advertise translation or localisation support that does not exist.

## Legal and compliance caution

"Global" is a positioning statement, not a compliance claim. Data-protection obligations differ by jurisdiction (for example GDPR in the EU/UK, and various national regimes elsewhere). This project does **not** claim compliance with any of them. The privacy page states what is collected and why, and marks unresolved items `TBD`. Legal review is required before launch. See [`../06-privacy-and-safety/PRIVACY-REQUIREMENTS.md`](../06-privacy-and-safety/PRIVACY-REQUIREMENTS.md).

## Review checklist for new copy

Before publishing any statement about reach, ask:

- [ ] Does it name a number? If yes, is it verifiable today?
- [ ] Does it imply members, partners, events or coverage that do not exist?
- [ ] Does it use a country, flag or map as decoration implying presence?
- [ ] Could a visitor reasonably read it as a promise of access, funding or employment?
- [ ] Is the tense correct — *is* for today, *planned* for the cohort, *aim* for ambition?
