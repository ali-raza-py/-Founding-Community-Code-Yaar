01Definition

What is the Founding Cohort?

The founding cohort is the first group of members in the Code Yaar community. It is deliberately small: one hundred places, filled through open applications rather than invitations.

Founding members are not beta testers with a fixed deliverable, and they are not customers. They are early participants who take part in practical activities as they become available, contribute feedback, and help establish a culture that later members will join.

Because the community is new, some activities and processes are still being designed. We say so rather than describing a programme that does not exist yet.

02Eligibility

Who should apply?

  • Students interested in programming and computer science.
  • Beginners who want to develop practical skills rather than collect courses.
  • Self-taught learners who have been working alone.
  • Aspiring developers preparing for a first role or a first serious project.
  • People who enjoy building projects and showing unfinished work.
  • Curious learners who want to collaborate instead of studying in isolation.
  • People who can participate consistently, even if that means a small amount of time.

Advanced technical experience is not required. If you are early in your learning and willing to try, you are the audience this cohort was designed for.

03Commitment

What do we expect from members?

  • Participate constructively and respect other members.
  • Share learning experiences, including the parts that did not work.
  • Give useful feedback when asked — specific, not just encouraging.
  • Attempt practical activities when they are available, at your own pace.
  • Communicate honestly, including when you cannot take part.
  • Contribute according to your realistic availability, not an imagined ideal.

There are no mandatory daily hours and no attendance requirements for live sessions. We would rather have an honest two hours a week than a commitment nobody can keep.

04Process

Selection process

Four stages, clearly stated. The review timeline is not published yet, because we have not measured our capacity to meet one.

  1. 01

    Application submission

    You complete the five-step form on this site. It asks about your background, your motivation and what you could contribute.

  2. 02

    Application review

    A person on the team reads your application. Selection considers motivation, willingness to learn, interest in practical work, collaboration and realistic availability — not existing skill level.

  3. 03

    Selection notification

    You are contacted by email with the outcome. The review timeline has not been published yet; we will not promise a date we cannot meet.

  4. 04

    Onboarding

    Selected applicants receive onboarding information: community guidelines, how to introduce yourself, and the first optional activity.

Submitting an application does not guarantee acceptance. Places are limited to one hundred founding members.

05Participation

Global participation

Applications are welcome from anywhere in the world. The founding cohort is being assembled from scratch, and we expect members to join from different regions and time zones.

Activities are designed to be asynchronous-first so that participation does not depend on attending a session at one particular local hour. Where live sessions happen, they will be optional and paired with an asynchronous path.

English is the working language of the site and the cohort. Preferred communication language is collected in the application so we can plan support, not to filter applicants.
