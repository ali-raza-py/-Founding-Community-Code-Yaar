The Cohort

It is the first 100 members of the Code Yaar community: a founding cohort of learners and aspiring developers who take part in practical activities, give feedback, and help set the tone for everyone who joins later. The cohort is being assembled now, so this is the beginning of the community rather than an established network.

No fee is being charged for the founding cohort, and members are not paid either. If any future programme has a cost, it will be stated clearly before anyone is asked to commit to it.

Yes. Applications are open worldwide, and activities are designed to work across time zones, mostly asynchronously. The community is new, so the founding cohort is the first international group rather than an existing global membership.

No. Beginners are the core audience. Curiosity, consistency and a willingness to learn matter more than experience. Being able to say what you want to learn is more useful to us than a long list of technologies.

No. University students, school students, bootcamp learners, self-taught developers and people outside formal education are all welcome. The application includes options for self-taught applicants and people who are not currently studying.

Applications are read by the team and considered on motivation, willingness to learn, interest in practical work, collaboration and realistic availability. Existing skill level, follower counts, institution names and country are not selection criteria. A review turnaround time has not been published yet.

There is no quota and no daily requirement. Most members should expect a few hours a week at most, and the application asks for your honest availability. Someone with two realistic hours is more useful to the community than someone who plans twenty and disappears.

Activities are designed to be asynchronous-first, with optional live sessions in rotating time windows where they happen. No activity should require attendance at one fixed local hour. A published activity calendar does not exist yet.

A certificate is not currently part of the founding cohort, and founding membership is not a certification or a credential. If that changes, it will be stated on this site and in the cohort details before anyone is asked to rely on it.

No. Code Yaar makes no employment, internship, scholarship or career-outcome promise. What the cohort offers is practice, peers, feedback and early involvement — not placement.
